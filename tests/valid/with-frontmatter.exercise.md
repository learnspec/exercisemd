### Part A — a statement section

A heading whose label does not end in `)` is a statement section: it
carries shared context, no points and no grading callouts.

### A.1)

A context node: it introduces material for the sub-questions below and
carries no grading callout of its own. A validator must not warn about a
missing rubric here.

### A.1.a)

State the value of $2 + 2$.

> [!answer] 4

> [!rubric]
> | Criterion | Points |
> |---|---|
> | Correct value | 2 |

### A.1.b)

Explain why the result does not depend on the order of the operands.

> [!solution]
> Addition is commutative on $\mathbb{N}$: $a + b = b + a$ for all
> $a, b$.

> [!expected]
> - The answer names commutativity, or states the property explicitly.

> [!rubric]
> | Criterion | Points |
> |---|---|
> | Commutativity named or stated | 2 |
