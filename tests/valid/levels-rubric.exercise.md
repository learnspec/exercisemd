# Levels rubric with anchors

## E1 · Essay

```exercise
points: 20
```

Discuss whether a map can ever be neutral.

> [!expected]
> - The essay takes a position and examines at least one objection to it.

> [!rubric levels]
> | Profile | Floor |
> |---|---|
> | Blank, or does not address the question | 1 |
> | No argumentation, no examples | 2 |
> | Poorly handled: weak plan, unsupported assertions | 6 |
> | Position, plan and examples present, but partial treatment | 10 |
> | Clear position, coherent plan, supported examples | 13 |
> | Strong: controlled argument, examples that do real work | 16 |
> | Outstanding: the terms of the question are themselves interrogated | 19 |
>
> Floors are minimums, not ceilings.

> [!anchor score:13]
> Takes a position and holds it, with two developed examples. The objection
> is named in one sentence but not examined. Conclusion repeats the
> introduction.
