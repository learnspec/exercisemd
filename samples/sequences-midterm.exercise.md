# Midterm — recursive sequences

> [!expected]

> not merely asserted; partial work is credited.

## E1 · A recursive sequence

Let $(u_n)$ be defined by $u_0 = 10$ and, for every natural number $n$:

$$u_{n+1} = 0.8\,u_n + 3$$

### a)

Prove by induction that $u_n \leq 15$ for every natural number $n$.

### b)

Let $v_n = u_n - 15$. Show that $(v_n)$ is geometric, and give its ratio
and first term.

### c)

Deduce an explicit expression for $u_n$, then the limit of $(u_n)$, and
interpret it.
